**context**：
> I needed to go into a space where I wouldn't have to think, and I was willing to pay for that.
> Disney World was the opposite of New York City. It was clean. It was sanitized. There were no surprises except good surprises.
**费曼一下**：把迪士尼当药——用一个「无意外、无杂质、被设计过」的环境，对冲现实里的不确定与焦虑。它和「为了开心去玩」是两件事——它是为了**停止思考**。Jade Dale 把这层意思推到极致：「In a world where I do increasingly feel unsafe… I've never felt worried about going to Disney.」
