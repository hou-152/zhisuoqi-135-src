## 来自《中国「竖屏短剧」如何征服世界》
- **context**：
	> Vivian, the producer, suspected that many viewers were young mothers with a busy, fragmented schedule, who might watch episodes as they waited for laundry or picked up a child from school. Cooper, the founder of Vertical Drama Love, agreed: 'There's just a lower capacity for attention, because everyday life is so hard.'
- **费曼一下**：微剧不抢"晚 8 点黄金时段"，而抢"等晾衣服的 90 秒"。把被传统电视忽略的女性碎片时间打包成新的内容基础设施。这是性别经济学 + 注意力经济学的交叉点：好莱坞放弃 rom-com，微剧用 60 集竖屏剧高效填满这个空白。
