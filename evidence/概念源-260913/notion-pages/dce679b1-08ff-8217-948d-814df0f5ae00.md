## 大规模监控（Bulk Surveillance）
**context**：
> "This line of thinking was prompted by one of Anthropic's 'red lines' that they declined to cross... the idea of 'no domestic bulk surveillance.'"
> "...it is already possible or it's not very far from interested parties being able to track your location at any time, if you have your phone on you or you're in a car or you're inside a city, in a 'bulk' rather than targeted way."
**费曼一下**：与「针对某个嫌疑人」的定向监控相对——不挑对象，先把所有人都记下来再说。技术让「全员记录 + 批量解读」的成本一降再降，于是「是否合理」这件事要被重新定义。文章整篇都在围着 bulk 这个字打转：什么时候它是必要恶，什么时候它是滑坡的开端。
