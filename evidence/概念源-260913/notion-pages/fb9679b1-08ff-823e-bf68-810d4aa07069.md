- **context**
	> "We have the digital divide, but in reverse, where in the nineties, people were scared that only the wealthy would have digital everything. But actually, digital everything is super cheap. Like, you basically have the same phone as Larry Page… now it's physical that's a premium product."
- **费曼一下**：90 年代的恐惧是"穷人用不上数字"，今天反过来——数字内容已经被极致通缩，从 Larry Page 到普通人用的是同一台手机、同一个 Wikipedia；真正变成稀缺品的是物理体验，于是 in-person 反而成为高价值信号。
- **意义**：解释了为什么 Network School、线下 meet-up、pen and paper 这类"反技术"场景现在反而重新变成资源，是 Human-Only Social Networks 发生的宏观背景。
