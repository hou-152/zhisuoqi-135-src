- **context**：
	> Instead of treating all earlier text as equally important, V4 compresses older information and focuses on the parts most likely to matter in the present moment, while still keeping nearby text in full so it does not miss important details.
- **费曼一下**：传统 attention 把过往所有 token 一视同仁，文本越长越贵。V4 的做法是：**远的压缩、近的保留全文、当下最相关的重点关照**。等于给模型加了一层"工作记忆 vs 长期记忆"的分层。
