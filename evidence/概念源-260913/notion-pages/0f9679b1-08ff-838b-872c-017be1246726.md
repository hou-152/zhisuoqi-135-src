## Step Change（阶跃变化）
- **context**：
	> "This really was a step change," Ethan Mollick told me. "For years now, we've been in an era of chatbots that mostly just *say* things. Now we've officially crossed into the era of agents that can actually *do* things."
- **费曼一下**：能力曲线不是平滑的，而是从一个台阶跳到下一个台阶。Mollick 用「say things → do things」概括这次跳跃：从只会「说」的 chatbot，跨入能「做」的 agent。一旦发生 step change，旧的市场判断（包括「AI 是泡沫」）就需要重写。
