- **Ostwald 阶梯规则 (Ostwald's Step Rule)**
	- **context**：
		> Ostwald's step rule suggests that systems transition to the closest and easiest-to-reach phase state rather than the most thermodynamically stable one — and that they sometimes then get stuck.
	- **费曼一下**：系统倾向于跳到"最容易到达"的相，而不是"最稳定"的相，而且常常就卡在那里——一种相变里的反直觉惯性法则。最易达到的状态，往往恰恰是最不稳定的。
