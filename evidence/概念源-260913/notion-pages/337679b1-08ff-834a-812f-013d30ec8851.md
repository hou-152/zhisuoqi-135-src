- **context**
	> "We've put capitalism on chain with cryptocurrency… The only thing that doesn't exist online is a binding vote… we restore rule of law with the rule of code."
- **费曼一下**：把"投票"升级成可执行的合约——anybody can vote for anybody（反 gerrymandering），当选者也被代码约束，违反 campaign promise 自动触发后果。从 *rule of law* 到 *rule of code*——民主从此不再依赖政治家自律。
- **举例**：Trump 许诺不打伊朗但打了——这种"选后不兑现"在现有机制里无代价；Social Smart Contract 要让这种违诺变得不可能。Balaji 提到将开源一套实现。
