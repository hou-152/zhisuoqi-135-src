- **context**
	> "I actually was an angel investor in Polymarket, but I did it because I actually don't really believe in prediction markets. I believe in verification markets. That is to say, to actually resolve whether a bet happened, you actually have to have the historical record of what happened."
- **费曼一下**：与 prediction markets 不同，verification markets 的难点不是"预测未来"，而是"用不可篡改的历史记录来裁决一件事是否真的发生过"。它把"算法共识"从金融延伸到任何可裁决的社会事实，是 Ledger of Record 的商业化应用层。
- **内部用例**：Balaji 身为天使提到企业可以用 prediction market 跟踪 feature ship 时间、bug 修复时间——唯一能让赌注生效的，就是事后的 verification 机制。
