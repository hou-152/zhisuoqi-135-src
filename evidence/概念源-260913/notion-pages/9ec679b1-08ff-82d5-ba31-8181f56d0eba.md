## context（来自上文）
> Poor sleep health (including sleep difficulties) has been linked to detrimental health conditions, including obesity and depression. The American Heart Association identified sleep health as a complex concept, highlighting the need for monitoring sleep quality in addition to duration.
## 费曼一下
一个多维概念——不只是「睡几小时」，还包含「是否容易入睡、是否能维持、醒来是否清爽」。AHA 把它列为复杂的、与心血管代谢健康直接相关的指标。
含义：如果只盯着 7 小时这个时长门槛，会漏掉一半信息。睡眠健康 = 数量（duration） + 质量（quality）的合体，而质量本身又有多个子维度。
