- **中子散射 (Neutron Scattering)**
	- **context**：
		> hydrogen is practically invisible to X-rays… So in addition to using X-rays, Bove's team adopted a different technique: They sent a stream of neutrons into the hot ice.
	- **费曼一下**：X 射线对氢原子几乎"看不见"；用中子束打进冰里，则能直接探到氢的位置和运动，从而判断分子是在振动还是在自转——发现塑性冰 VII 的关键技术。
