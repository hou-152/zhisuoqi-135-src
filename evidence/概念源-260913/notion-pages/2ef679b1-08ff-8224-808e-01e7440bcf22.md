- **context**：
	> The rise of artificial intelligence is built on factories of data and computation, so-called *data centers*. These large warehouses of networked computers on racks require substantial energy to operate and water for cooling, in addition to physical square footage on the landscape.
- **费曼一下**：AI 的物理基础设施。一栋装满成排联网计算机的大仓库 —— 吃电、吃水（用来冷却）、占地。所有「AI 烧水」话题，本质都是这些仓库带来的话题。
