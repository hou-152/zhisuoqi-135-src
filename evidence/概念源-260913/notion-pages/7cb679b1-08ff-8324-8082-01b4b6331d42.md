**context**：
> If the heart of software brain is the idea that thinking in the structured language of code can make things happen in the real world, well, the heart of lawyer brain is that thinking in the structured legal language of statutes and citations can also make things happen.
**费曼一下**：相信只要把法条像代码一样写好，社会就会按你写的那样运行。和软件脑长得几乎一样——都依赖**先例**（case law / 代码库），都用**结构化语言**指挥复杂系统。Larry Lessig 在 2000 年《Code and Other Laws of Cyberspace》里早就点出过这种同构。正是这种诱人的相似让两类人共鸣，也让两类人一起忽略一件事：法律的核心不是确定性，是模糊性。
