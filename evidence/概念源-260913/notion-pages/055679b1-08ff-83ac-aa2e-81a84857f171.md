> OpenAI's ad platform has two halves. On the ChatGPT side, the backend injects structured `single_advertiser_ad_unit` objects … On the merchant side, a tracking SDK called OAIQ runs in the visitor's browser and reports product views back to OpenAI. The two are tied together by Fernet-encrypted click tokens, four of them per ad.
**费曼一下**：传统搜索广告的闭环是「关键词 → 落地页 → pixel → 转化」。OpenAI 把这个范式搬到了对话场景：「**对话主题（contextual targeting）→ 内嵌广告单元（SSE 注入）→ 内置 webview 跳转 → 商家页 OAIQ 上报**」。**关键创新不是「投了广告」，而是「把对话变成了一个可归因的渠道」——这是 LLM 公司商业化最关键的一块拼图。**
