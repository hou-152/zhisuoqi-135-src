- **context**
	> "You apply to your country at age eighteen, and that's how we restore democratic choice where you have consented to the government that governs you because you have a choice, a practical choice of a thousand of them."
- **费曼一下**：当美国蓝红州都事实上变成"一党国"（de facto one-party state），民主已经名存实亡。修复方式不是再选一次，而是给"退出权"——同时有 physical exit 和 digital exit，把世界拆成 1000 个可以选择加入的社群。"选国家"比"选总统"更能恢复 *consent of the governed*。
- **配套机制**：与 Social Smart Contract 一起，构成 Balaji "techno-democracy" 的治理层。Network School（新加坡）就是早期实验场。
