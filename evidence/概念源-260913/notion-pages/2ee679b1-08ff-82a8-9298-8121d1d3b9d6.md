- **冰相 (Phases of Ice)**
	- **context**：
		> Ice is the name for any phase of water that is solid and crystalline, meaning that it has a repeating molecular structure. Over the past decade, computer simulations have predicted tens of thousands of possible forms of ice.
	- **费曼一下**：所谓"冰"，不止冰箱里那一种，而是水分子在不同温度和压强下、按不同方式重排凝固成的整个晶体家族。目前实验室已观察到 20 多种，模拟预测的更是上万种——每一种都是一个独立的相。
