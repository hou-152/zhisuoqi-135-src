AI will force mathematics to move away from the overly formal proofs of the French Bourbaki style and instead focus on the ideas. For instance, with respect to the Navier-Stokes proof.


The proof has time running in reverse but keeps the idea of time moving forward from an initial condition to a final condition. The Millennium problem requires the initial velocity to be well-defined, smooth, and divergence-free. By reversing time, the singularity matches the final condition instead of the initial condition, and this is also forbidden if time is reversed.


Velocity is distance over time, x/t. At t = 0, velocity has a singularity with x/0 and is not defined. We cannot use this initial condition because it does not satisfy a smooth, well-defined initial velocity.


The AI uses the definition tau = (1-t) and describes it as a countdown to a singularity. Jean Leray first did this ‘trick’ in his foundational 1934 paper on fluid dynamics.


The infinitesimal dtau is the mirror opposite of regular time, dt. The infinitesimal dtau = -dt. As t increases, tau decreases. You can prove this by taking the time derivative of tau.


dTau/dt = (d/dt)(1-t) = -1


dtau/dt = -1


dtau = -dt


The Euler equations are time reversible. They are identical to the Navier-Stokes equations but have zero viscosity. Using dtau in Euler and ending with a blowup is identical to starting with a forbidden blowup as an initial condition, due to time symmetry. No proof of this type can ever prove that the velocity in Euler’s equations diverges.


Navier-Stokes has viscosity, so it is not time reversible. Energy dissipates; this is like entropy defining the arrow of time. The AI proof going backward in time does not equal a proof going forward in time. The two cannot be equal. If they were equal, we would be working with Euler’s equations, not Navier-Stokes.


The AI proof setting dtau = -dt over the chosen interval invalidates the proof of finite time blowup for both the Euler and Navier-Stokes equations.


Navier-Stokes has a symmetry due to Gauss, what goes in must come out and a conservation law due to Noether’s theorem.


Saying an area of infinitely small volume goes to infinite velocity as energy goes to zero is literally a proof that nothing travels at infinite speed. Similarly, L = I × ω, and I = mR^2; at zero radius, the angular momentum is zero. No mass density is traveling at an infinite speed. The ideas are still defined. There is no contradiction. Zero multiplied by infinity is zero.
