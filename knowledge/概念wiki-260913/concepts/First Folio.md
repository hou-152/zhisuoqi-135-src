# First Folio

> 1623 年出版的莎士比亚剧作合集，若无它，凯撒、暴风雨、麦克白等半数剧作将失传。

## 费曼一下

1623 年莎翁去世 7 年后，朋友和同台演员凑钱把他的作品整理印成的一本"死后合集"。750 本起印，今天仅存 235 本（其中 Folger 图书馆一家就藏了 82 本）。

## 原文 context

> *A few years ago now Shakespeare's First Folio, a mammoth collection of his work compiled by friends and fellow players, crossed the century mark for the fourth time. An estimated 750 copies were originally published in 1623, seven years following his death.*<br><br>*If we didn't have this book, we wouldn't care about Shakespeare at all. Half of the plays would have just been lost. We wouldn't have Julius Caesar, The Tempest, Macbeth.* — Emma Smith
